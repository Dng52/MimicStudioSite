Who are we?

We are a small group dedicated to the art of creating. Our goal is to collectively create games and other forms of entertainment that we ourselves would love.

How do we work?

Everyone at Mimic Studio has a direct stake in the work. Any profit made from a project is distributed amongst the contributors. Teams are small, focused, and dedicated to their art.

Anti-AI. No substitute for human creativity.

Generative AI is a pervasive, slop producing scourge that pretends to be a substitute for the human mind. It strips meaning and purpose from art by stealing humanity's greatest collective efforts. Mimic Studio is anti-AI and does not use AI "art" in any way.
